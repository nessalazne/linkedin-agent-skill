# Story Bank

What you have actually done, in numbers, with names and dates. `voice.md`
holds how you sound. This holds what you have to say.

`/li-interview` copies this file to `~/.claude/linkedin/story-bank.md` and
fills it by interviewing you. You can edit that copy by hand any time. It
stays on your machine: it is not in the repo, so a push can't carry it.

## 1. Timeline

Where you have worked and what changed at each stop. "March 2024" beats "last
year", because a post can anchor on it.

- (role, company, from-to, what you were actually responsible for)

## 2. Receipts

Numbers you can state without checking. One odd, precise figure with a named
referent is the strongest sign a person wrote the post.

Good: "cut deploy time from 22 minutes to 9, team of four, Q2 2025".
Useless: "improved efficiency significantly".

- (number, what it measures, when, whose it is)

## 3. Shipped

Things that exist because you worked on them.

- (what it was, your part in it, what it cost or returned)

## 4. Turning points

Where you changed your mind, or the plan changed under you.

- (what happened, what you believed before, what you believe now)

## 5. Scars

What went wrong and what it taught you. A scar is a cost you paid, not a view
you revised.

- (what broke, the real cost, what you do differently)

## 6. Positions

Opinions you would defend in a room that disagreed, and what holding them
costs you.

- (the claim, who disagrees, why you hold it anyway)

## 7. Recurring stories

The ones you already tell out loud. You know they land.

- (the story in two lines, and the point it makes)
